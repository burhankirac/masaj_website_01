    What is InTouch® massage?

    InTouch® massage is built on the foundations of classic massage and adds modern techniques from body therapy developed over the past decades. It is taught at the InTouch® Massageschule in a professional training of 10 modules and 21 training days (about 214 teaching units), completed with an exam. InTouch® massages are for stress relief, relaxation and well-being, as wellness and preventive care, not for treating illness.

    Questions & answers

    Where does the massage take place?

    At your home, hotel or office in Neukölln and neighbouring districts. Further away? Just ask. For now I can only come if you have a massage table at your place. I bring fresh towels and oil. Sessions in a massage room in Neukölln are coming soon.

    What do I need to prepare?

    Your massage table, set up in a quiet, warm room with a little space to walk around it.

    What should I wear?

    You undress as far as you feel comfortable; underwear stays on. You are covered with towels the whole time, and only the area being massaged is uncovered.

    Can I tell you what I like?

    Yes, please. At the start we agree on pressure, oil or no oil, music or silence, and whether you'd like to talk or rest quietly. You can change your mind at any time.

    Which oil do you use?

    My standard is pure, unscented sweet almond oil. If you like, I can use a scented natural massage oil instead: a calming one with lavender, or a warm, floral one with ylang-ylang and bergamot. If you prefer a lighter, less oily feel, I can use a massage lotion that absorbs well and washes out easily. After the massage you'll have a fresh towel to remove any excess oil yourself. A light film may still remain, so it's best not to wear delicate clothes (e.g. silk) right afterwards. Please tell me when you book if you have a nut, sesame or other allergy, are sensitive to scents or essential oils, or are pregnant. I'll then use unscented, nut-free jojoba or grapeseed oil. For the face I use a separate, gentle face serum; if you have very sensitive facial skin, please let me know.

    I'm pregnant or have a health condition. Can I still book?

    Please mention it when you book. At your first session you fill in a short health form. In some cases, such as fever, acute inflammation or recent surgery, a massage isn't possible or I adapt it. If you're unsure, ask your doctor first.

    How do I pay?

    After the massage, in cash or by bank transfer. No VAT is charged (§ 19 UStG).

    How can I cancel or move my session?

    Free of charge up to 24 hours before, by email or phone. For later cancellations or if you're not there, 50 % of the price is due.
