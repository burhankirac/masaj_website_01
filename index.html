    What is InTouch® massage?

    InTouch® massage is built on the foundations of classic massage and adds modern techniques from body therapy developed over the past decades. It is taught at the InTouch® Massageschule in a professional training of 10 modules and 21 training days (about 214 teaching units), completed with an exam. InTouch® massages are for stress relief, relaxation and well-being, as wellness and preventive care, not for treating illness.

    Questions & answers

    Where does the massage take place?

    At your home, hotel or office in Neukölln and neighbouring districts. Further away? Just ask. For now I can only come if you have a massage table at your place. I bring fresh sheets and oil. You can also come to a massage room in Neukölln; you get the address with my confirmation.

    What do I need to prepare?

    Your massage table, set up in a quiet, warm room with a little space to walk around it.

    Why a short video call before the first visit?

    It's a friendly way to get to know each other before I come to your home. In about 5 minutes we can say hello, you can ask any questions, and I can see your massage table and the room, so everything is ready on the day. It's optional, and we can do it on WhatsApp, Signal or Telegram.

    What should I wear?

    You undress as far as you feel comfortable; underwear stays on. You lie between soft, freshly washed sheets, and only the area being massaged is uncovered.

    Can I tell you what I like?

    Yes, please. At the start we agree on pressure, oil or no oil, music or silence, and whether you'd like to talk or rest quietly. You can change your mind at any time.

    Which oil do you use?

    My standard is pure, unscented sweet almond oil. If you like, I can use a scented natural massage oil instead: a calming one with lavender, or a warm, floral one with ylang-ylang and bergamot. If you prefer a lighter, less oily feel, I can use a massage lotion that absorbs well and washes out easily. After the massage you can wipe off any excess oil with the sheet. A light film may still remain, so it's best not to wear delicate clothes (e.g. silk) right afterwards. Before we start, I'll ask you about allergies (for example to nuts or sesame) and sensitivity to scents. I always bring unscented, nut-free jojoba or grapeseed oil as well. For the face I use a separate, gentle face serum; if you have very sensitive facial skin, please let me know.

    I'm pregnant or have a health condition. Can I still book?

    Please mention it when you book. At your first session we go through a short health form together. In some cases, such as fever, acute inflammation or recent surgery, a massage isn't possible or I adapt it. If you're unsure, ask your doctor first.

    How do I pay?

    After the massage, in cash or by bank transfer. No VAT is charged (§ 19 UStG).

    How can I cancel or move my session?

    Free of charge up to 24 hours before, by email or message. For later cancellations or if you're not there, 50 % of the price is due.

    Do you offer gift vouchers?

    Not for sale, but regular clients get one: after 4 visits you receive a free massage to give away. See Come 4 times, give 1 away.
